# Brand and content specification

## Audience and promise

- Audience: Japanese small-business owners and hiring managers unfamiliar with SNS or web production.
- Promise: Make the people, work, and atmosphere of a company visible through short videos so candidates can understand it before applying.
- Official name: `ミルハイア by CONNECTED MATERIAL`
- Brand line: `見える会社は、選ばれる。`
- Primary CTA: `無料で相談する` or a confirmed equivalent.

## Voice

- Use plain Japanese and concrete verbs.
- Avoid jargon, hype, fear, long paragraphs, and AI-generated-sounding abstractions.
- Write for scanning: one conclusion per heading, up to three bullets where possible, and detail in FAQ or accordions.
- Separate facts, interpretations, and service claims.

## Recommended page story

1. Header and concise CTA
2. Hero with promise, brief explanation, and real-looking work imagery
3. Six recognizable hiring problems
4. Structural cause: job listings disappear and do not preserve company understanding
5. Candidate behavior: people check people, work, and culture before applying
6. Solution declaration: Miruhaia visualizes the real company through short video
7. Three 9:16 sample videos: interview, work/site, team/culture
8. Support scope: strategy, planning, filming, editing, posting, analysis
9. Content themes and path from view to application
10. Easy operating model and reusable content value
11. Position for small companies trying SNS recruitment for the first time
12. Three plans
13. Optional add-ons or campaign only when confirmed
14. Four-step onboarding and FAQ
15. Final CTA and company information

## Plans

### 15万円｜AI動画生成プラン

- Minimal support
- Produce videos at volume with AI
- Do not infer contract period or quantity

### 20万円｜素材撮影・単発撮影プラン

- Film primarily once at the beginning
- Recombine the captured material with other person/material assets
- Use AI video as a hybrid
- Do not infer contract period or quantity

### 30万円｜定期撮影プラン

- 12-month contract
- Film every two months
- Source instruction says `計8本の動画を投稿`
- Do not rewrite `計8本` as monthly or annual until confirmed

## Publication-blocking inputs

- Contact form URL or delivery endpoint
- Privacy-policy URL
- Exact company information
- Tax inclusion/exclusion for fees
- Contract period and video quantities for 15/20万円 plans
- Meaning of `計8本` for the 30万円 plan
- Final campaign terms if shown
- Three final videos and publication permission evidence
- Final verified statistics and source URLs

Keep these in a single configuration module. Continue building with clearly marked non-public placeholders, but do not claim launch readiness.

## Color and layout

- Base 70%: `#FFFFFF`, `#FAF8F3`
- Main 20%: `#6051C8`, `#493AAE`, `#EEEAFE`
- Accent 8%: `#FFD84D`
- Brand detail 2%: `#D64B4B`
- Body text: `#12152F`
- Max content width: 1200 px
- Section spacing: 112–144 px desktop, 72–96 px mobile
- Text: Noto Sans JP with Japanese system fallbacks
- Cards: 24 px radius; photos/video: 32–40 px radius; soft shadows only
