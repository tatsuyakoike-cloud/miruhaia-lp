# Quality gates and current references

## Required visual checks

- Primary logo has no white rectangle, baked background, broken outlines, or excessive whitespace.
- White/ivory dominates; violet supports hierarchy; yellow appears only on decisions, numbers, and CTA emphasis.
- No black neon section, slide screenshot, dense comparison table, fake social UI, or long text over photography.
- Plan differences and the next action are understandable without reading every sentence.
- Desktop video cards align; mobile shows one usable card at a time.
- 320 px has no horizontal page overflow.

## Required functional checks

- All links have real destinations or are blocked from production.
- Menu, FAQ, video controls, and form work with keyboard and touch.
- Three approved videos load with matching posters and no autoplay audio.
- Form states include errors, success/failure, consent, and double-submit protection.
- No console error, 404, unresolved import, broken asset, or expired campaign.
- Build, lint, type check, and tests pass.

## Factual checks

- No unsupported number or superiority statement.
- Statistics display source name, publication date/year, and URL.
- Price tax status and contract/video quantities match approved facts.
- Publication permission is recorded for each real company/person video.
- Do not use 砂建 or highly exposed advertising examples.
